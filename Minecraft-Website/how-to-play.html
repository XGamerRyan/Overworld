09 / HOW TO PLAY · YOUR FIRST WORLD

Find a tree.
Make a plan.

New to Minecraft? Start with a mode, make a world, learn what the controls do, then gather enough supplies to get through your first night. Take the steps at your own pace.

START WITH AN EDITION

Pick the version for your group.

Java and Bedrock are developed separately. The supplied guide associates Java with PC and more mod/server customization, and Bedrock with cross-platform play on supported devices.

THE JAVA ROUTE

Consider it if your group plays on PC.

The source guide associates Java Edition servers with PC and notes its mod customization. Confirm device and version requirements before you buy, install mods or join a server.

THE BEDROCK ROUTE

Consider it for supported cross-platform play.

The source guide describes Bedrock as supporting cross-platform play on mobile, console and PC. What works together depends on the devices and game versions your group uses.

Check current product and account requirements on the official Minecraft site ↗ before purchase or sign-in.

THE FIRST-WORLD CHECKLIST

One step at a time.

Open each step for a quick primer. The exact controls and menus depend on edition, device and settings.

01Choose your edition and game mode.

Survival asks you to gather and manage supplies. Creative provides room to build freely. Adventure focuses on exploring player-made experiences; multiplayer joins others’ worlds. The names and available modes can depend on edition and world setup.

Meet the mobs →
02Create a new world.
Open the world-creation menu, give the world a name, choose the mode and check its settings. Keep your first world simple; you can always start another adventure later.
03Get comfortable with the controls.
On a computer, basic play commonly uses a keyboard and mouse; console and mobile use their own control layouts. Check the in-game controls menu for your device and adjust it before heading out. Your layout can differ from default bindings.
04Gather a few starting resources.
Break nearby blocks and collect wood as a basic first material. Use what you gather to craft simple tools. Pay attention to your surroundings while you collect.
05Build somewhere safe before night.
Make a basic shelter or find a protected spot. The source guide’s survival checklist puts a first shelter high on the first-night list; stay alert to hostile mobs in dark areas.
06Keep an eye on food and supplies.
Hunger and survival resources matter in Survival mode. Find food and make tools for mining and crafting; Creative mode removes many survival pressures.
07Explore caves and distant places carefully.
When you are ready, travel farther, mine resources, and learn the route back. The Nether is a hazardous dimension accessed with an obsidian portal; the End has its own challenges and is associated with the Ender Dragon.
08Build your next project.
Try Redstone and automation, use enchantments, switch to Creative, join friends, or simply make a more ambitious shelter. There is no required single path through the game.

How to Play Minecraft

(For Beginners)

Minecraft is a sandbox game that allows players to explore, build, survive, and create in a blocky, procedurally generated world. Whether you’re new to the game or just need a refresher, here’s a complete guide on how to play Minecraft.

1. Getting Started
Choose Your Edition

Minecraft is available in two main editions:

Java Edition: Primarily for PC, with more customization options and mods.

Bedrock Edition: Available on multiple platforms (PC, consoles, mobile) and allows cross-platform play.

Create an Account

Purchase and download Minecraft from the official site or your device's store.

Log in with your Mojang or Microsoft account.

Game Modes

When starting Minecraft, you can choose one of the following modes:

Survival: Gather resources, build shelter, and survive against mobs like zombies and skeletons.

Creative: Access unlimited resources and fly around the world to build anything you want without worrying about health or hunger.

Adventure: Play custom maps with specific challenges, where you may need tools to interact with the environment.

Hardcore: A more challenging version of Survival with permanent death (Java Edition only).

2. Starting Your First World
Create a New World

From the main menu, click Singleplayer (Java) or Play (Bedrock).

Select Create New World.

Customize settings like world name, difficulty, and game mode.

Basic Controls

Movement: Use WASD (PC), joystick (console), or touch controls (mobile).

Interact/Use: Right-click (PC) or the appropriate button for your device to open chests, place blocks, etc.

Attack/Break Blocks: Left-click (PC) or the attack button for your device.

Jump: Spacebar (PC) or the jump button.

Inventory: E (PC) or corresponding button for your platform.

3. Survival Mode Basics
Step 1: Gathering Resources

Punch Trees: Use your hands to punch trees and collect wood. Wood is a fundamental resource for crafting tools and shelter.

Craft Tools: Open your inventory, convert wood into planks, and make a crafting table. Use the crafting table to create basic tools like a pickaxe, axe, and shovel.

Step 2: Building Shelter

Dig or Build: Create a basic shelter using dirt or wood to protect yourself from hostile mobs (zombies, skeletons) that spawn at night.

Light It Up: Craft torches using coal and sticks to prevent mobs from spawning near your shelter.

Step 3: Food and Hunger

Hunt or Farm: Kill animals (e.g., cows, pigs, chickens) for food or grow crops like wheat and carrots.

Cook Food: Use a furnace to cook raw meat, which restores more hunger points.

Step 4: Mining and Crafting

Dig Deep: Mine stone and ores like coal, iron, and diamonds. Use better tools (crafted from these materials) to mine faster and access rare resources.

Craft Upgrades: Gradually upgrade tools and weapons for efficiency and protection, such as iron or diamond swords and armor.

Step 5: Exploring

Travel: Explore the world for new biomes, villages, and structures like temples, dungeons, and strongholds.

Combat: Learn to fight mobs using swords, bows, and shields.

4. Advanced Gameplay
The Nether

Build a Nether Portal using obsidian to access the Nether, a dangerous dimension filled with lava, hostile mobs, and unique resources.

Enchanting

Craft an Enchanting Table and use experience points to enchant tools, weapons, and armor for special abilities.

The End

Locate a stronghold to activate an End Portal. Travel to the End to fight the Ender Dragon, Minecraft’s final boss.

Redstone and Automation

Experiment with redstone to create automated systems like farms, doors, and traps.

5. Creative Mode

Creative Mode removes the survival aspect, allowing you to focus on building and designing without restrictions.

Access Blocks: Open the inventory to access all items and blocks in the game.

Fly: Double-tap the jump button to fly, allowing you to build massive structures easily.

6. Multiplayer
Joining a Server

Select Multiplayer from the main menu.

Add a server by entering its IP address or join public servers like Hypixel or Mineplex.

Playing with Friends

Use LAN (Local Area Network) to play with friends on the same network.

Create or join a Realms server (subscription-based) for a private multiplayer world.

7. Tips for Success

Always carry torches and food.

Avoid mining straight down to prevent falling into lava.

Stay aware of hostile mobs, especially Creepers that can silently explode.

Experiment with crafting recipes to discover new items.

Explore different biomes for unique resources and challenges.

8. Beyond Basics

Try custom maps and mods to enhance your gameplay.

Participate in mini-games and roleplay on servers.

Build ambitious projects like cities, castles, or automated farms.

Create your own adventures and share them with the Minecraft community.

Minecraft is a game of limitless creativity and exploration. Whether you’re surviving your first night, building intricate structures, or battling the Ender Dragon, there’s always something new to learn and explore. Enjoy the journey!
