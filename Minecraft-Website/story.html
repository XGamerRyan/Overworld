01 / THE STORY · FROM ALPHA TO OVERWORLD

A small idea.
A world that kept growing.

Minecraft’s history is a story of steady development, changing hands, and a game that gave its players room to add chapters of their own.

THE TIMELINE

One update at a time.

The original guide tells a story that starts with Notch’s early Java project and grows into an ongoing, player-influenced world.

2009
THE BEGINNING

A world takes shape in Java

Swedish game developer Markus “Notch” Persson began creating Minecraft as a Java-based sandbox project. The first public alpha build arrived on 17 May 2009—an early invitation for players to explore and build.

2009–2011
PUBLIC DEVELOPMENT

Players join the process

The game continued to develop through its public early builds. New systems and ideas accumulated, while players began forming the creative community that would become part of Minecraft’s identity.

2011
FULL RELEASE

The first full release

Minecraft reached full release on 18 November 2011. The source guide notes that Persson later left Mojang and development moved to Jens “Jeb” Bergensten.

2014
A NEW OWNER

Mojang joins Microsoft

Microsoft acquired Mojang and the Minecraft intellectual property in 2014 for US$2.5 billion, according to the supplied source history.

OVER THE YEARS
CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT

The world keeps expanding

Updates added blocks, items, creatures, biomes, structures, dimensions and gameplay features. Java and Bedrock development, and feedback from the community, are part of the continuing story.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND IT

From Persson to
a wider team.

Minecraft was originally created by Markus “Notch” Persson using Java. After the 2011 full release, Persson left Mojang and Jens “Jeb” Bergensten took over development. In 2014, Microsoft acquired Mojang and Minecraft’s intellectual property.

Today, the game’s ongoing development includes Java and Bedrock editions, while its players continue to influence how the Minecraft community builds, plays, and shares.

The Story of Minecraft

Minecraft is a 2009 sandbox game developed and published by Swedish video game developer Mojang Studios. Originally created by Markus "Notch" Persson using the Java programming language, the first public alpha build was released on 17 May 2009. The game would be continuously developed from then on, receiving a full release on 18 November 2011. Afterwards, Persson left Mojang and gave Jens "Jeb" Bergensten control over development. In the years since its release, it has been ported to several platforms, including smartphones, tablets, and various video game consoles. In 2014, Mojang and the Minecraft intellectual property were purchased by Microsoft for US$2.5 billion. Minecraft is the best-selling video game of all-time, with over 300 million copies sold and nearly 170 million monthly active players as of 2025.
